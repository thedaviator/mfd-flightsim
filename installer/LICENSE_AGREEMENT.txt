MFD BRIDGE AND MFD DISPLAY FIRMWARE
Use at your own risk - please read before installing

1. FOR FLIGHT SIMULATION ONLY

MFD Bridge and the MFD display are for entertainment and practice with flight
simulators (Microsoft Flight Simulator, X-Plane). They are NOT certified avionics.
NEVER use them for real-world flight, navigation, weather avoidance, terrain avoidance
or any other purpose where an error could cause injury, death, or damage to property.

The navigation, airport, weather and terrain information they show comes from free
third-party sources and from your simulator. It may be incomplete, out of date or
wrong.

2. USE AT YOUR OWN RISK

Updating the display's firmware, over WiFi or USB, may leave it unusable if the update

5. NETWORK

MFD Bridge opens TCP ports 8080 and 8082 and sends a UDP announcement on port 8081 on
your local network, so the display can find it. It connects to the internet for
weather, METARs, airport data and terrain tiles. It collects no personal data and
sends nothing to the authors.
