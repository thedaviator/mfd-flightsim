Data and online services
------------------------

Airports, runways, frequencies, navaids
                          OurAirports, public domain
                          https://ourairports.com/data/
Weather radar             RainViewer API, used under its terms of use. Radar data:
                          RainViewer.  https://www.rainviewer.com/api.html
Lightning                 NOAA GOES-R Geostationary Lightning Mapper, U.S. Government
                          work (public domain), via NOAA Open Data Dissemination on AWS
                          https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-goes/
METARs                    NOAA / National Weather Service, aviationweather.gov, public
                          domain  https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/
Terrain elevation         Terrain Tiles (Mapzen / Amazon Web Services). Elevation data
                          from SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1, USGS 3DEP and other sources; see
                          https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Data from these services may be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and is for flight
simulation only.
